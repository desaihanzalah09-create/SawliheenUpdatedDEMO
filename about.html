About Sawliheen Publications

Preserving and propagating pristine Islamic heritage

Our Foundation & Vision

Alhamdulillah, with the help and grace of Allah Ta’ala, Sawliheen Publications has been formed with the aim of propagating various subject matters pertaining to the Deen of Islam in accordance to the Ahlus Sunnah Wal Jam’ah.

In an age filled with vast amounts of information, discerning authentic guidance has never been more critical. Inspired by the profound words of Muhammad bin Sireen (Rahimahullah): “Indeed, knowledge is an integral part of Deen, therefore be careful whom you take your religious guidance from,” our institution stands as a reliable beacon for seekers of sacred knowledge.

We aim to bridge traditional scholarship with accessible literature, covering Fiqh, Aqeedah, Tazkiyah (spiritual purification), and contemporary moral guidance.

Our Vision

To disseminate genuine Islamic literature that educates, purifies hearts, and firmly anchors generations upon the pristine teachings of classical scholarship.

Our Mission

To publish reliable treatises, booklets, and scholarly works addressing daily spiritual, social, and religious needs under qualified scholarly supervision.
